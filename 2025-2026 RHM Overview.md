## Non-Credit RHM Modules
Flexible micro-credentials for industry partners with two delivery models.

### Online, Self-Paced Modules (Front-Line Employees)
- Target: Front-line employees, apprentices, seasonal staff.
- Length: Under 60 minutes; pause and resume anytime.
- Topics: Guest services/service vs. hospitality; workplace professionalism and communication.
- **Free option (WDC Partnership Model)**: Requires reporting SSNs through IDOL portal; available until **March 15, 2026**.
- **Standard model**: $25 per seat per training topic for organizations not reporting SSNs.

### In-Person, Interactive Group Training (Leadership/Management)
- Target: Supervisors, managers, high-potential leaders.
- Delivery: In-person facilitated training (minimum 60 minutes; longer as needed).
- Topics: Conflict to Coaching (difficult conversations); The Coaching Manager (coaching strategies); open to other in-demand topics.
- **Free bundled option (WDC Partnership Model)**: Organizations using online modules can add **one** in-person group training free if they report SSNs through DOL portal; valid until **March 15, 2026**.
- **Standard model**: $1,500 per in-person training for up to 25 people for organizations not reporting SSNs.

## RHM Internship Requirements: RHM 493 Student-Led Leadership Project
- Functions as the academic capstone within a **paid employment setting**; students secure part-time or full-time roles first.
- Students execute a **Student-Led Leadership Project** within their job function during RHM 493.
- **Value to organizations**:
  - Builds a talent pipeline by keeping students in committed paid roles.
  - Demonstrates proactive leadership through identifying gaps/opportunities and delivering measurable outcomes.
  - Requires project goals/outcomes aligned with organizational needs; supervisor signs off on completion.
- **Key partnership requirements**:
  - Placement must be **paid employment**; compensation should cover living needs if outside Treasure Valley.
  - Structured traditional internships are acceptable if clearly outlined; employment contract remains separate from the course.
  - Housing must be provided or subsidized for non-local seasonal interns.
  - Supervisor sign-off is required to validate the leadership project.

## Training Overview: Conflict to Coaching
- Estimated time: ~2 hours including breaks.
- Focus: Turning conflict into growth through six phases of resolution and applying five conflict styles (Collaborating, Competing, Compromising, Accommodating, Avoiding).
- Emphasis on creating a positive atmosphere, setting ground rules, and choosing appropriate time/place for conversations.
