## 1. Overview and Foundations
### 1.1 Purpose of this playbook
- Provide a repeatable system for programs to leverage COBE Marketing channels efficiently and consistently.【F:RHM Playbook.txt†L4-L21】
- Reduce duplication, clarify requirements, and capture best practices that already work for RHM/NP/+Business programs.【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L1-L12】

### 1.2 Who this is for
- **Marketing staff**: Apply channel specs, approve content, and maintain calendars.
- **Student workers**: Follow checklists for drafting, submitting, and scheduling content.
- **Faculty/Program leads**: Supply timely inputs (photos, quotes, event info) and review messaging for accuracy.【F:RHM Playbook.txt†L13-L18】
- **Leadership/partners**: Use for planning, budgeting, and oversight; assign owners for gaps.

### 1.3 COBE brand overview
- **Mission/positioning (RHM focus)**: Deliver industry-aligned hospitality education and training that converts students into workforce-ready leaders and offers flexible micro-credentials for employers.【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L1-L103】
- **Personality/tone**: Professional, solutions-oriented, and collaborative; emphasize student success, employer partnership, and accessibility (best practice recommendation – adjust as needed for COBE).
- **Key value propositions**:
  - Career fairs and mixers that connect employers with COBE talent.【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L1-L15】
  - Flexible online/self-paced and in-person leadership trainings for frontline to management teams.【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L15-L38】
  - Paid, project-based RHM493 internships that produce measurable outcomes for host organizations.【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L39-L64】

### 1.4 Target audiences
- **Prospective undergraduates**: Seeking flexible credentials and career pathways; want clear value, community, and outcomes. COBE promises stackable certificates and industry exposure.【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L15-L38】
- **Prospective graduate students/continuing education**: Need leadership and management upskilling; COBE offers in-person facilitation tailored to supervisors/managers.【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L26-L38】
- **Current students**: Need advising on certificates, events, and internships; COBE provides news, social posts, and email updates with clear CTAs and deadlines.【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L1-L34】
- **Alumni**: Interested in professional development and networking; promote mixers, leadership modules, and partner trainings.【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L15-L38】
- **Employers/industry partners**: Need talent pipelines and training; COBE offers fairs, paid project-based interns, and free/paid training bundles.【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L1-L38】【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L39-L64】

### 1.5 Core messaging pillars
1. **Career-readiness and leadership** – COBE programs convert learning into measurable workplace impact through paid leadership projects and targeted training.【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L39-L64】
   - “Students lead real projects that deliver measurable value for host organizations.”
   - “Supervisors sign off on outcomes, ensuring relevance and accountability.”
2. **Flexible learning formats** – Online self-paced modules plus in-person facilitation to meet frontline and management needs.【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L15-L38】
   - “Complete modules in under 60 minutes; pause and resume anytime.”
   - “Add one in-person leadership workshop to deepen skills.”
3. **Employer partnership and pipeline** – Career fairs, mixers, and bundled trainings connect employers to vetted talent and upskilling options.【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L1-L15】【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L35-L38】
   - “Meet COBE students at targeted mixers before major fairs.”
   - “Bundle self-paced and in-person training to accelerate team readiness.”
4. **Accessible collaboration** – Clear submission rules, lead times, and approvals reduce friction for departments and student orgs.【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L1-L34】【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L39-L58】
   - “Submit requests two weeks ahead to cobe-info@boisestate.edu with all assets.”
   - “Complete accessibility training before editing WordPress content.”

## 2. Channels and Tactics
### 2.1 Instagram
- **Role**: Build student interest and event awareness with quick visuals (posts, stories, reels). Best for current students and prospects.
- **Primary audiences**: Current students, prospective undergrads, alumni for highlights.
- **Content types**: Square posts (1080x1080), stories/reels (1080x1920); reuse assets sized per channel list.【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L17-L24】
- **Cadence**: 3–4 posts/week during peak recruitment; stories around events.
- **Creative/copy**: Short, energetic captions; strong CTA to register or learn more; ensure Boise State brand compliance.【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L24-L34】
- **Example assets**: Shuttle stop poster resizing to 1080x1920; event square graphic for Localist/IG.【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L19-L24】【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L80-L89】
- **Workflow/ownership**:
  1. Program provides graphic, caption, link ≥2 weeks in advance to cobe-info@boisestate.edu.【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L24-L34】
  2. COBE Marketing reviews for brand, schedules; student worker can draft/schedule.
  3. Approval by marketing manager for sensitive items.
- **Measurement**: Impressions, saves, shares, link clicks, event RSVPs.
- **Links/templates**: [Social Media Calendar Template.xlsx](Social%20Media%20Calendar%20Template.xlsx), [Social Media Calendar Example.xlsx](Social%20Media%20Calendar%20Example.xlsx).

### 2.2 LinkedIn
- **Role**: Professional storytelling, employer partnerships, alumni highlights, Online BBA community building.【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L7-L12】【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L149-L154】
- **Primary audiences**: Employers, alumni, graduate/continuing-ed prospects.
- **Content types**: 1920x1080 banners for events; articles, student spotlights, employer partnerships.【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L19-L24】
- **Cadence**: 2–3 posts/week; increase around fairs/mixers.
- **Creative/copy**: Professional, outcome-focused; include employer value props and links to landing pages.
- **Workflow/ownership**: Program submits assets and blurb; COBE Marketing approves and posts via central or program page.
- **Measurement**: Click-throughs to landing pages, employer inquiries, event registrations.
- **Links/templates**: Use campaign brief template (Section 3.2) for major employer campaigns.

### 2.3 Email marketing
- **Role**: Drive event attendance, applications, and program inquiries; supports recurring student event newsletters.【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L28-L34】【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L64-L75】
- **Primary audiences**: Current students, prospects, employers (via targeted lists).
- **Content types**: Event announcements, certificate highlights, employer opportunities.
- **Cadence**: Student events email early each month; second send mid-month as needed.【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L64-L75】
- **Creative/copy**: Clear subject lines with date/time; concise blurbs; single CTA button.
- **Workflow/ownership**:
  1. Request inclusion ASAP with subject “STUDENT ORG REQUEST” and full event details to cobe-info@boisestate.edu.【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L28-L34】
  2. Marketing compiles newsletter; approvals by marketing manager.
- **Measurement**: Open rate, CTR, registrations, inquiries.
- **Templates**: See Section 7.1 for email skeleton and sample blurbs.

### 2.4 Website and landing pages
- **Role**: Primary source of truth for program details, forms, and news; host interest forms and Localist events.
- **Primary audiences**: All segments; emphasize accurate, accessible content.
- **Requirements**:
  - WordPress access requires completion of web accessibility compliance training (60–90 minutes).【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L39-L46】
  - Post-training, editors receive Intro to WordPress resources and must follow security and accessibility standards.【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L47-L60】
- **Workflow/ownership**:
  1. Submit web updates to cobe-info@boisestate.edu with subject “STUDENT ORG REQUEST” and clear edits.【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L1-L8】
  2. Marketing/web team reviews for brand/accessibility; publish or provide editor access after training.
- **Measurement**: Pageviews, form submissions, time on page; ensure UTM tracking on campaigns.
- **Links/templates**: [RHM Playbook.docx](RHM%20Playbook.docx) for initial scope; Localist event outline in notes (Section 7.6).

### 2.5 Events and in-person activations
- **Role**: High-intent engagement for recruitment and employer branding (career fairs, mixers, panels).【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L1-L15】【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L80-L89】
- **Primary audiences**: Employers, current students, prospects.
- **Content types**: Posters (24x31, 24x35), rack cards (3.5x8.5), banners (728x90), shuttle stop posters, table signage.【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L17-L24】【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L62-L64】
- **Cadence**: Align with academic calendar—orientation, mid-semester fairs, late-semester panels.
- **Workflow/ownership**:
  1. Reserve rooms (registrar for MBEB first floor; Gail Puccetti for others).【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L53-L55】
  2. Submit posters/ads ≥2 weeks ahead with FDCC if printing needed.【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L18-L24】
  3. Coordinate digital screens (MBEB 1920x1080, SUB 768x1366, Housing 1920x1080, Library 1280x720) via listed contacts.【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L37-L49】【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L20-L24】
  4. Post events to Localist and COBE news; include photo and registration link.【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L64-L89】
- **Measurement**: RSVPs, attendance, employer follow-ups, QR scans.

### 2.6 Partnerships and employer outreach
- **Role**: Build talent pipelines and training revenue; promote fairs, RHM493 projects, and training bundles.【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L1-L38】【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L39-L64】
- **Primary audiences**: Hospitality employers, industry associations, campus partners.
- **Content types**: One-pagers on training models; emails with fair dates/fees; case highlights from RHM493 projects.
- **Cadence**: Quarterly outreach; ramp before fall/spring fairs and WDC training deadlines (before March 15, 2026).【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L23-L38】【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L98-L103】
- **Workflow/ownership**: Program lead drafts brief; COBE Marketing reviews positioning; leadership approves pricing/terms.
- **Measurement**: Employer sign-ups, training seats sold, project placements.

## 3. Planning and Campaigns
### 3.1 Annual and semester planning
- Anchor plans to academic milestones: move-in/orientation, early-month student newsletters, mid-semester career fairs/panels, registration windows, graduation.
- Build 2–3 month rolling calendar; lock event assets 3–4 weeks out; submit promotions 2+ weeks ahead.【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L1-L34】

### 3.2 Campaign framework
| Field | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign name | Clear, audience-specific (e.g., “Fall Hospitality Career Mixer”). |
| Background/problem | Context, prior results, constraints. |
| Objective(s) | SMART goals (e.g., 100 RSVPs, 30 employer leads). |
| Target audience(s) | Segment plus key needs. |
| Key message & offer | One-sentence value prop + CTA. |
| Channels | Instagram, LinkedIn, email, web, events, employer outreach. |
| Timeline & milestones | Kickoff, asset due, approvals, launch, follow-up. |
| Budget | Note paid ads/printing; reference RHM budget ranges if relevant.【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L31-L38】 |
| KPIs & success criteria | Per-channel metrics + conversion goal. |

### 3.3 Content calendar
- **Structure**: Weekly view for tasks; monthly for themes/events; semester for major milestones.
- **Example week**: 3 IG posts (event teaser, student story, reminder), 1 LinkedIn employer post, 1 email newsletter build, 1 Localist post.
- **Example month**: Week 1 newsletter, Week 2 IG reel + event posters submitted, Week 3 LinkedIn alumni story, Week 4 event recap.
- **Templates**: [Social Media Calendar Template.xlsx](Social%20Media%20Calendar%20Template.xlsx), [Social Media Calendar Example.xlsx](Social%20Media%20Calendar%20Example.xlsx).

### 3.4 Coordination with internal partners
- Hold monthly planning with program leads (RHM, NP, +Business) to align messaging and assets.【F:RHM Playbook.txt†L4-L18】
- Loop COBE Marketing early with channel needs; share briefs and drafts in shared drive (best practice recommendation – adjust as needed for COBE).
- For career services events, coordinate with COBE Career Services and submit digital signage requests to contacts listed.【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L37-L56】

## 4. Brand, Creative, and Copy Guidelines
### 4.1 Brand voice and tone
- **Voice**: Confident, welcoming, action-oriented; focuses on student/employer outcomes (best practice recommendation – adjust as needed).
- **Do**: Use clear CTAs, highlight measurable benefits, keep language inclusive.
- **Don’t**: Overload with jargon; omit required details (dates, links, contact).

### 4.2 Visual identity
- Use Boise State brand standards; include logos where appropriate except MBEB screens (logo already present).【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L9-L16】
- Core sizes by channel/event: 1080x1080, 1080x1920, 1920x1080 for social; posters 24x31/24x35; rack card 3.5x8.5; banners 728x90; shuttle posters 24x35.【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L17-L24】【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L62-L64】
- Outline edges on light-background digital ads; keep text minimal for rotations.【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L9-L16】

### 4.3 Copywriting guidelines
- **Prospective students**: Lead with career outcomes, flexibility, and stackability; include registration/app links.
- **Employers**: Emphasize ROI, training bundles, and pre-vetted talent; include fair dates/fees.【F:2025-2026 RHM Overview.txt†L1-L38】
- **Current students**: Clear event details, deadlines, and benefits (e.g., resume boosts, networking).【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L28-L34】【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L64-L75】
- Subject/headline patterns: “Join [event] on [date] to [benefit]”; “Upskill your team with [module] before [deadline].”
- CTA language: “RSVP on Handshake,” “Book a training,” “Request inclusion via cobe-info@boisestate.edu.”

### 4.4 Accessibility and inclusivity
- Complete required web accessibility training before editing WordPress; ensure content meets accessibility standards.【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L39-L58】
- Provide alt text for images, captions for video, and high-contrast color choices (best practice recommendation – adjust as needed).
- Use inclusive language; avoid assumptions about background or ability.

## 5. Processes, Workflows, and Roles
### 5.1 Roles and responsibilities
- **Marketing manager**: Approves strategy, sensitive content, budgets; ensures brand compliance.
- **Coordinator/student worker**: Drafts posts, builds emails, schedules content, gathers assets.
- **Program lead/faculty**: Supplies event info, quotes, approvals for program accuracy; leads employer outreach.
- **Web editor**: Maintains pages after completing accessibility training.【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L39-L58】

### 5.2 Content production workflow
1. **Intake**: Submit request with assets/brief to cobe-info@boisestate.edu ≥2 weeks ahead (subject “STUDENT ORG REQUEST”).【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L1-L34】
2. **Draft**: Coordinator creates copy/graphics per channel specs.
3. **Review**: Program lead checks accuracy; marketing manager checks brand/accessibility.
4. **Finalize**: Incorporate edits; prepare scheduled posts/emails.
5. **Publish**: Schedule per cadence; post Localist/website updates.
6. **Report**: Capture metrics; log learnings (see Section 6.4).

| Step | Owner | Tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intake | Requestor/Program lead | Email to cobe-info@boisestate.edu, campaign brief |
| Draft | Coordinator | Design tools, calendar templates |
| Review | Program lead / Marketing manager | Shared docs, brand checklist |
| Finalize | Coordinator | Scheduler, email platform, WordPress |
| Publish | Coordinator/Manager | Social scheduler, Localist, web CMS |
| Report | Coordinator/Analyst | Reporting templates |

### 5.3 Approvals and review
- Required for: website changes, paid ads, event emails, external partnerships.
- Target turnaround: 3–5 business days; submit earlier for print/signage (2–4 weeks).【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L1-L24】

### 5.4 Request intake process
- Email cobe-info@boisestate.edu with subject “STUDENT ORG REQUEST.” Include: channel(s), dates, copy, graphics (correct specs), links, FDCC for printing, and desired start/end dates.【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L1-L34】
- For web edits, specify exact text/URL to change; for events, include full description and registration link.【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L1-L34】【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L80-L89】

### 5.5 File and asset management
- Store assets in shared drive folders by campaign; include final graphics in required sizes.
- Name files with topic_start-end-date (e.g., “CareerFair_2025-10-01_1920x1080.jpg”).【F:Promotion Resources.txt†L9-L16】
- Maintain links to templates (Section 7.6) and keep current versions dated.

## 6. Measurement, Reporting, and Optimization
### 6.1 Key metrics and definitions
| Metric | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Reach/Impressions | Unique users/total views on social posts. |
| Engagement rate | (Reactions + comments + shares) / impressions. |
| CTR | Clicks divided by sends or views for email/ads. |
| RSVPs/Registrations | Event sign-ups via Handshake/Localist/forms. |
| Applications/Inquiries | Program interest form submissions. |
| Employer leads | Employers requesting training/fair participation. |

### 6.2 Reporting cadence
- **Weekly**: Social/email performance snapshot; flag tests.
- **Monthly**: Channel roll-up vs. goals; student newsletter summary.【F:Kate Watson Marketing director notes.txt†L64-L75】
- **Per campaign**: Launch + two-week recap; include learnings.
- **Semester**: Outcome summary (RSVPs, applications, employer deals).

### 6.3 Reporting templates
- **Channel report**
  | Channel | Period | Posts/Sends | Reach | Engagement/CTR | Top content | Notes |
- **Campaign report**
  | Campaign | Objective | KPI target | Actual | Drivers | Next actions |
- **Semester/annual**
  | Term | Key campaigns | Leads/RSVPs | Applications | Employer partners | Budget used | Recommendations |

### 6.4 Experimentation and learning
- Run small A/B tests (subject lines, thumbnails, CTAs) on email/social.
- Log results in a simple test log:
  | Date | Channel | Hypothesis | Variant A | Variant B | Result | Next step |
- Roll successful variants into future calendars; document in shared drive.

## 8. Governance, Policies, and Risk Management
