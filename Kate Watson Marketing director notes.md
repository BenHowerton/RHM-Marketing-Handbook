# Kate Watson Marketing Notes

Working notes on building a holistic COBE marketing playbook, channel requirements, deliverables, and communication threads.

## Purpose and Outcomes
- Create a sustainable, scalable process that reduces duplicated effort for COBE Marketing and programs.
- Package proven marketing approaches into a simple, hand-off-ready system.

## Planned Deliverables
- **Workflow “Blue Book”**: Step-by-step guidance for collaborating with COBE Marketing.
- **Templates & checklists**: Campaign briefs, content request forms, rollout schedules.
- **Channel map & rules**: What belongs on newsletters, social, website, email lists, LinkedIn groups (incl. Online BBA), and how to submit.
- **Resource hub**: Shared folder or page for process guides, brand assets, contacts, and examples.
- **Best practices & KPIs**: Tactics that work with simple metrics (engagement, CTR, leads).
- **Roles & approvals**: Ownership, program preparation, and lightweight approval workflow to keep the team informed.
- **Sustainability**: Where living documents reside, maintenance expectations, and onboarding for new programs.

## Newsletter Cadence and Content Guidance
- Student events email typically goes early each month, with a possible mid-month send if volume is high.
- Feature certificate blurbs periodically; vary messaging to avoid fatigue.
- Provide square-friendly photo, short attention-grabbing paragraph with value props, and CTA link.
- Certificates and events can also be shared on Localist to reach broader calendars.

### Certificate Blurb Examples (for newsletters/social)
- **Resort & Hospitality Management Certificate**: Seven-course, fully online program covering management, operations, and finance for hotels, resorts, venues, and tourism. Highlights national recognition, stackability, and flexibility for working professionals.
- **Nonprofit Management Certificate**: 12-credit interdisciplinary program covering governance, fundraising, budgeting, and evaluation; emphasizes purpose-driven skills and easy degree fit.

## Localist Event Outline Example
