# Promotion Resources

Guidelines and contacts for submitting COBE marketing and promotion requests with required lead times and specs.

## Lead Time and Submission Basics
- Send all promotion requests at least **2 weeks in advance**; submit earlier for printing and digital screens.
- Email **cobe-info@boisestate.edu** with the subject **"STUDENT ORG REQUEST"** for every request type.
- Include clear details: channel, dates, copy, graphics, start/end dates, and FDCC when printing is needed.

## COBE Website Updates
- Email cobe-info@boisestate.edu with subject "STUDENT ORG REQUEST."
- Specify exactly what to change on the organization webpage or student organization listing.
- Events open to all students can be added to the COBE events page; submit full details **2 weeks before** the event.

## Digital Display Ads (MBEB Touchscreens)
- Submit JPG or PNG at **1920 x 1080 px, 72 ppi**.
- No Boise State logo required (already on screen); outline edges if ad has white borders and keep text minimal.
- Name files with topic and start/end dates and include those dates in the email.
- For 2-week rotations, send graphics **4 weeks before** the event; for 1-week rotations, send **3 weeks before**.
- Ads rotate every 15 seconds and must follow Boise State brand standards.

## COBE News
- Email cobe-info@boisestate.edu with subject "STUDENT ORG REQUEST."
- Share news, accomplishments, or announcements for possible feature on the COBE news site.

## Printing Posters
- Send poster printing requests **2 weeks in advance** to cobe-info@boisestate.edu with subject "STUDENT ORG REQUEST."
- Create posters in InDesign or similar; paper size **24 x 31** (plasti-core boards). Printer leaves white margins.
- Include printing timeline and FDCC funding string in the email.
- Borrow poster boards and easels from the Dean's Office; return after use.
- Costs: **Uncoated 24 x 31 – $8**; **Semi-gloss 24 x 31 – $12** (student pricing discount).

## Social Media Posts on COBE Accounts
- Submit at least **2 weeks in advance** to cobe-info@boisestate.edu with subject "STUDENT ORG REQUEST."
- Provide on-brand graphics, captions, and any organization handles; note whether the post fits LinkedIn, Instagram, or Facebook.

## Email Communication
- Send requests **as soon as possible** to cobe-info@boisestate.edu with subject "STUDENT ORG REQUEST."
- Events may be included in COBE upcoming events emails; schedules vary and inclusion is not guaranteed.
- Provide: requestor email, engaging event description, date/time/place, registration link (if required), and photo.
- Emails cannot include attachments.

## Flyers in MBEB
- Flyers may be hung on bulletin boards only (not walls, windows, doors). Remove flyers after the event.

## Campus Digital Screens
### Library Digital Screens
- Size **1280 x 720 px**, JPG; contact **Luan Teed**.

### Rec Center Digital Screens
- Size **1920 x 1080 px**, JPG; contact **Jared Cox**.

### On-Campus Housing Digital Displays
- Size **1920 x 1080 px**; submit one-page PPT or PDF (landscape preferred) via housing marketing submission form.

### Student Union Building (SUB) Promotions
- Submit all promo requests and materials via SUB promotional request form; questions: **subpromos@boisestate.edu**.
- **Toilet Talk**: 1000 x 1000 px JPG; submit by last Thursday of the month for next month’s rotation.
- **Social Media**: Content may be shared on SUB Facebook/Instagram (posting not guaranteed).
- **Digital Displays**: 768 x 1366 px JPG for dining screens; 1920 x 1080 px JPG for Info Desk screens/kiosks; submit 1.5 weeks before desired start.

### Other Campus Promotions
- Contact specific buildings/departments for permission and posting rules; practices vary by location.
- Reference shared flyering route when planning.
