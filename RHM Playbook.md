# RHM Playbook

Summary of the initial scope and objectives for building a COBE-aligned marketing playbook for RHM, NP, and +Business programs.

## Purpose
- Build a sustainable, scalable marketing process that reduces duplicated effort and supports COBE Marketing.
- Capture best practices that ease workload for the marketing team and other programs.

## Main Objectives
- **Immediate action**: Collaborate directly with COBE Marketing to actively market RHM, NP, and +Business programs.
- **Final deliverable**: Produce a best-practice playbook that documents effective, low-friction ways for programs to market through COBE channels.

## Why This Matters
- Programs often lack awareness of all COBE-managed channels (social, newsletters, websites, email lists, LinkedIn groups).
- Many departments have no dedicated marketing staff and need clear, repeatable processes.
- COBE Marketing controls core channels, but efforts are fragmented; the playbook provides a shared roadmap.

## Playbook Perspective
- Write from the point of view of a **Program Manager or Department Lead** with limited marketing resources.
- Focus on what a department needs to submit and how to engage COBE Marketing efficiently.

## Key Content to Capture
- List of COBE Marketing channels available to programs.
- Requirements for submitting content (image sizes, copy length, lead time).
- Necessary deliverables to provide to COBE (photos, quotes, data).

## Final Deliverable Guidance
- Best-practice guide backed by proof of concept showing successful collaboration with COBE Marketing for RHM, NP, and +Business.
- Start in a simple format (slide deck or basic document) that can be refined into a website or formal document later.
